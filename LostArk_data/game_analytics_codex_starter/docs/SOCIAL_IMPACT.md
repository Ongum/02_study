# Social value and safeguards

## Intended contribution (not achieved outcome)
- Make recurring accessibility, technical stability, moderation and fairness-related complaints easier to spot.
- Help community teams prioritize issues by urgency and evidence instead of raw volume alone.
- Improve visibility into language-specific concerns without using nationality stereotypes or unfairly profiling users.
- Reduce manual triage workload while retaining human review and appeal/override.

## Proof required
For each proposed benefit specify affected stakeholders; baseline; before/after measurable outcome (alert precision, detection latency, reviewer minutes saved); dataset and labeling method; risk of false alarm/underrepresented language groups; privacy protections. Until evaluated, write **hypothesis**. Never claim game quality, safety or company decisions were actually improved merely because a dashboard exists.

## Ethical constraints
Public comments may contain personal details or harmful content: keep raw data access controlled, minimize identifiers, redact public displays, document retention and deletion. Do not train or expose user-level profiling without legitimate basis. Respect Steam API limitations and attribution. Include small-language-sample uncertainty and human-in-the-loop triage.
